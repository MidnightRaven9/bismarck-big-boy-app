OUR STORY

70 years of comfort food, community, and nostalgic flavor.

FROM THE BISMARCK BIG BOY ARCHIVES
1954

The Beginning

Harley McDowell opened Big Boy in Bismarck after eating at one on a business trip. That same year he became the fourth KFC franchisee — and "Colonel" Harland Sanders and McDowell ultimately became close friends.

1978

The Rothenberger Years

McDowell sold the Bismarck restaurants to Stan and Bonnie Rothenberger. He wasn't the highest of five bids — McDowell chose him for "his established reputation for honest business and quality food service."

2017

Where We Are Now

Chad Wachter, a lifelong Bismarck resident and Big Boy fan, took over. He added an easier-to-read menu board, a second drive-through exit, and a park with picnic benches — while keeping every recipe exactly the same.

TODAY

The Sole Survivor

Regional locations once existed in downtown Bismarck, Fargo, Minot, Dickinson, and Medora. The original Bismarck restaurant remains the sole survivor.

MISSION

At Bismarck Big Boy, we serve up timeless favorites with a side of tradition, offering great food, fast service, and a place where memories are made, one order at a time.

VISION

To honor and grow our Big Boy tradition by being North Dakota's go-to destination for comfort food, community, and nostalgic flavor — where every generation feels at home.

FROM THE OWNER

"We are proud to be part of many memories that center around family and food. Food from Big Boy brings back fond family memories for me. We made improvements to the property, but the food is the same. There was no need to mess with perfection. Our food will taste the same way it did when you ate it with your family, on that first date, or on some other special day in your life." — Chad Wachter, Owner
